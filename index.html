About me

I am a Cybersecurity student at Assumption University with a strong interest in network security, cyber risk management, and governance, risk, and compliance (GRC). I focus on how organizations protect their infrastructure while building structured security frameworks that support reliable and resilient operations. I maintain a 4.0 GPA and have earned the ISC2 Certified in Cybersecurity (CC) certification.

Alongside my studies, I gain hands-on experience through security labs, cybersecurity club activities, and maintaining a home server environment where I work with system configuration, network access control, and secure infrastructure management. My long-term interest is in the integration of cloud security and artificial intelligence to strengthen enterprise network and operational security, with the goal of helping organizations build scalable and resilient security systems.
